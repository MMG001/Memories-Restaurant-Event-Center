Soulfull Sunday Soul Food Buffet in Minneapolis

Every Sunday · $37.99 · Drink included

Soulfull Sunday is a $37.99 soul food buffet in Minneapolis for families and after-church groups, with roast, smothered and fried chicken, catfish, classic sides and dessert.

$37.99

Includes a drink & going through the buffet 3 times.

Every Sunday · Call 612.223.3679 for buffet hours
