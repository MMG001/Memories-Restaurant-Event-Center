Taco Tuesday Birria in North Minneapolis

Every Tuesday · 11AM – 8PM

Taco Tuesday at Memoire's is all-day birria in North Minneapolis for taco fans and the lunch crowd: tacos, quesadillas, egg rolls and birria pizza, each with consomé for dipping, 11AM – 8PM.
