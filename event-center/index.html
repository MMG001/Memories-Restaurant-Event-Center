Event Center & Private Party Venue in North Minneapolis

Every type of event. Food and DJ provided.

The Memoire's event center is a private party venue in North Minneapolis for hosts planning birthdays, receptions, repasts and corporate events, with food from our kitchen and a DJ provided.

What We Provide

Food

Catering from the Memoire's kitchen, drawing on our menu of soul food, steaks, salmon and vegan dishes.

DJ

A DJ to set the mood, from dinner music to a full dance floor.

The Space

A modern room with a full bar and warm lighting. See the gallery.

How Booking Works

  1. Send a rental request with your date, guest count and services.
  2. Our team contacts you with availability and pricing.
  3. Plan your menu and music with us.
  4. Celebrate. We handle the food and the DJ.

Pricing depends on your date, the number of guests, the menu and the services you choose. Ask us for a quote.
