Memoire’s Restaurant & Event Center (“Memoire’s,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us — for example your name, phone number, email address and event details when you send a message or rental request. Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

How We Use It

  • To respond to your questions, reservations and event inquiries.
  • To plan and provide services you request.
  • To improve our website and guest experience.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers who help us operate the website or our business, or when required by law.

Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Maps and Google Fonts, which may collect data under their own privacy policies.
