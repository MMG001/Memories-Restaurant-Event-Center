Our Story

Soul Food, Served With Elegance

Memoire’s brings Southern comfort and a refined night out together under one roof on Minneapolis’ North Side — slow-cooked classics, hand-cut steaks and a room built for celebrating.

Whether you’re here for a quick lunch, a Saturday brunch with friends or a celebration in our event center, we’re here to make it memorable.
