Frequently Asked Questions About Memoire’s, Minneapolis

Answers for guests visiting Memoire's in North Minneapolis: reservations, hours, menus, vegan options and private events. Still have a question? Contact us or call 612.223.3679.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for any visit and required for Brunch Vibez on Saturdays. Call 612.223.3679 to book a table.

Where is Memoire’s located?

We are at 2201 N 44th Ave, Minneapolis, MN 55412, in North Minneapolis. See the visit page for the map and directions.

When are lunch and dinner served?

The Lunch Special runs Wednesday – Friday, 11AM – 3PM. Dinner is served Wednesday – Friday, 3:30PM – 8PM.

What happens each week?

Taco Tuesday runs 11AM – 8PM, the lunch special runs midweek, Brunch Vibez is every Saturday 11AM – 6PM and the Soulfull Sunday buffet is every Sunday.

When is happy hour?

Happy hour is Tuesday – Friday · 11AM – 1PM and 6PM – 8PM.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. Dinner has Meatless Memories and the Dirty Cabbage Skillet, lunch has a $16 vegan option, and Taco Tuesday has Veggie Birria.

What does the Sunday buffet include?

The Soulfull Sunday soul food buffet is $37.99 and includes a drink and going through the buffet 3 times.

Can I host a private event at Memoire’s?

Yes. The event center hosts every type of event, and we provide the food and the DJ. Send a rental request or call us.

Do you have a kids menu?

At Brunch Vibez, the Kids Meal ($12) comes with 2 mini waffles or 1 pancake.
