Who We Are

Made for Memories

Memoire’s Restaurant & Event Center is a place to slow down, gather and celebrate.

Our kitchen serves soul food classics and hand-cut steaks — fried catfish, smothered chicken, mac & cheese, greens and yams, alongside our Memoire’s Cut and Boss Cut NY strips. Every week brings something to look forward to: Taco Tuesday, a weekday lunch special, Brunch Vibez on Saturdays and our Soulfull Sunday buffet.

Beyond the dining room, our event center is ready for every type of occasion, with food and a DJ provided by our team.
