About Memoire’s, a North Minneapolis Soul Food Restaurant

Memoire's Restaurant & Event Center is a North Minneapolis soul food restaurant and event center for diners who want a relaxed meal or a night to celebrate.

Made for Memories

Our kitchen serves soul food classics and hand-cut steaks: fried catfish, smothered chicken, mac & cheese, greens and yams, alongside the Memoire’s Cut and Boss Cut NY strips on the dinner menu.

Every week brings something to look forward to: Taco Tuesday, a weekday lunch special, Brunch Vibez on Saturdays and our Soulfull Sunday buffet.

Beyond the dining room, our event center hosts every type of occasion, with food and a DJ provided by our team.

The Memoire's Experience

Soulful Cooking

Southern comfort, steaks and salmon, plus vegan dishes so everyone has a seat at the table.

Weekly Vibes

Birria Tuesdays, Saturday brunch, the Sunday buffet and happy hour Tuesday through Friday.

Event Center

A warm, modern room for private parties and gatherings of every kind.

Hospitality

An elegant room, a friendly team and a night out that feels like home.

Our Space

Wood floors, black-and-white finishes, a full bar and big windows. See more in the gallery, plan your trip on the visit page, or contact us.
