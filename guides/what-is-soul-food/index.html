What Is Soul Food? Classic Dishes & Where to Try Them

Soul food is the traditional cooking of African Americans, rooted in the American South. This guide is for diners who want to know the classics before sitting down for dinner or the Sunday buffet.

Where the name comes from

The cooking is centuries old. The term “soul food” became widely used in the 1960s, alongside “soul” music and culture.
