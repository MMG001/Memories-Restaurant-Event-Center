How to Plan a Private Event: A Simple Checklist

Planning a private event starts with five decisions: date, guest count, budget, food and music. This checklist is for anyone hosting a birthday, shower, repast or work event and getting ready to request a venue.

1. Pick a date and a backup

Popular weekends book first. Have a second date in mind when you contact a venue.

2. Estimate your guest count

A close number drives the food order, seating and price. Count confirmed guests plus a small buffer.

3. Set a budget range

Venue cost usually depends on the date, the guest count, the menu and add-ons like bar service or decor.

4. Choose food and music

Decide between a buffet or plated meal, and whether you want a DJ. At Memoire’s, food and a DJ are provided, and the menu draws on our restaurant menu.

5. Build a simple timeline

WhenTask
6–8 weeks outBook the venue; send invitations
3–4 weeks outConfirm the menu and music
1 week outFinal guest count to the venue
Day ofArrive early; enjoy your guests

What to send a venue

Event type, date, start and end time, guest count, budget range and the services you need. Our rental request form asks for exactly these. Prefer brunch for a birthday? See Brunch Vibez.
