Privacy Policy for Memoire’s, Minneapolis

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Memoire's Restaurant & Event Center (“Memoire's,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send: your name, phone, email and event details when you use the contact form or rental request. Form submissions are processed by Cognito Forms and delivered to our inbox. Our hosting provider may record basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To answer questions, reservations and event inquiries.
  • To plan and provide the services you request.
  • To improve our website and guest experience.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers who help run the website or our business, or when the law requires it.

Third-party services

This site embeds Google Maps, which may collect data under Google’s own privacy policy.

Your choices

Ask us to update or delete information you sent by calling 612.223.3679. See also our terms.
